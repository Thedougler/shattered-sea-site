## Overview

Brilliantly colored amphibious humanoids native to tropical rainforests and fetid swamps. Vivid skin hues simultaneously signal lethal toxicity to predators and denote rigid social caste within Grung civilization. To touch a Grung uninvited is to invite a miserable death.

In the Shattered Sea, Grung are found primarily in the interior of [[the_verdant_teeth]] island cluster. See [[the_grung]] for the full society, caste structure, and faction details.

**Creature Type:** Small Humanoid · **Speed:** 25 ft · **Life Span:** ~30 years

## Species Traits

**Arboreal.** Climb Speed equal to Speed. Proficiency in the Perception skill. No Darkvision — Grung eyes are adapted to bright canopy light, not darkness.

**Aquatic Nature.** Can breathe air and water. Must submerge in water for at least 1 hour per 24-hour period. Failing this gains 1 Exhaustion level that can only be removed by completing the required immersion.

**Standing Leap.** Long Jump 25 ft, High Jump 15 ft — with or without a running start.

**Sticky Tongue.** Prehensile tongue, 10-foot reach.
- *Snatch* (Bonus Action): pull one unattended object of own size or smaller within 10 ft into hand or adjacent space.
- *Lash* (replaces one Attack): attack roll using Strength or Dexterity. On a hit, target is Grappled (escape DC = 8 + PB + ability mod). Tongue occupied while Grappling.

**Poisonous Skin.** Immunity to Poison damage and the Poisoned condition. Proficiency with Poisoner's Kit.
- *Venom Coating (L1, Bonus Action)*: coat one melee weapon or up to five pieces of ammunition. Uses = Proficiency Bonus; recharges on Long Rest. Target makes Constitution save (DC = 8 + PB + Con mod) or takes Poison damage equal to twice Proficiency Bonus and is Poisoned until start of its next turn.
- *Toxic Secretion (L5)*: skin becomes persistently toxic. Creatures hitting with unarmed strikes, natural weapons, or bare-hands Grapple attempts must make the same Constitution save or take Poison damage equal to Proficiency Bonus and be Poisoned. Can spend 1 minute collecting secretions to fill one vial of Basic Poison — after which passive contact effect is suppressed until a Short or Long Rest.

## Caste by Skin Color

Skin color is determined at birth and defines social caste for life. See [[the_grung]] for full protocols and the political implications of the Gold caste's fractured island-by-island structure.

| Color | Role |
|---|---|
| **Gold** | Clan leaders, spiritual authority |
| **Red** | Scholars, memory-keepers, poison-crafters |
| **Orange** | Artisans, builders, weapon-makers |
| **Purple** | Warriors, hunters, law-keepers |
| **Blue** | Scouts, messengers, handlers of slaves |
| **Green** | Labourers (lowest freeborn caste) |

## Skin Contact

Non-combat skin-to-skin contact in a social setting is not casual to a Grung — it initiates a mating sequence. Grung who have lived among other species develop awareness of the difference; those who haven't find landwalker physical affection genuinely baffling and mildly disturbing.
