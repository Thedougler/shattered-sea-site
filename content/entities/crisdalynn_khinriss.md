## Overview

A crow of the [[sentinels_of_the_eyrie]], permanently banished from [[the_high_eyrie]] at age 14 in 1493DR for treating her assigned pilgrimage as a joke. The pilgrimage: map [[the_drowned_maw]] from inside a storm. The banishment stands until the task is complete. She is the youngest crow in a tradition that rarely sees fifty.

[[master_kyzil]], who personally vouched for her readiness, approved the banishment.

## The Dreidel

Before she left the Eyrie, [[master_kyzil]] gave her his dreidel — a Kensei teaching tool centered on balance and motion. She has been carrying it since 1493DR.

## The Red Lady

Following her banishment, she met [[delmar_fisk]] and shipped aboard the [[red_lady]] as navigator and crow. Served for one year and ten months — the closest thing to a substitute home she found. She was already aboard when Fisk assembled [[fisks_fleet]].

## The Sinking

On Day −5, Umberlee's retribution came as fog so thick the water was invisible from deck — surrounding all five ships simultaneously. A rogue trench opened and plunged all five ships 80 feet down. She dived from the crow's nest, grabbed Fisk, and flew straight up. She had only risen 50 feet when the water closed in; momentum and buoyancy carried them both to the surface. They heard Umberlee's laughter on the wind, then her rage, as the ships went under. She is one of two survivors of five ships and their crews. The decision to grab Fisk rather than simply escape is notable. ^[inferred]
