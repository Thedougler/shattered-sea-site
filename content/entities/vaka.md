## Overview

A loose community of Grung defectors and freed former slaves at [[port_tidefall]], built over eight years by [[felix_aho]]. Not a formal organization — a network of people finding their footing outside [[the_verdant_teeth]], anchored by Félix's fish stall and his willingness to receive anyone who shows up. ^[inferred]

## Structure

Deliberately informal. [[felix_aho]] is the founding elder and most connected member, but leadership is not the point — the point is that the place exists, that people in it know they are not alone, and that someone is not running from anything anymore. ^[inferred]

## Significance

The Vaka represents proof of concept: Grung can leave, survive outside Grung territory, and build something that isn't about raiding or hierarchy. This is politically significant given the Gold caste's investment in lower castes believing there is no alternative. ^[inferred]
