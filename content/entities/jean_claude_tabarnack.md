## Overview

A **Blue caste** [[grung]] from [[sorn]] in [[the_verdant_teeth]]. Blue caste role: scouts, messengers, and handlers of slaves. [[pell]] — the gnome slave killed in reprisal for his desertion plans — was someone he was caste-assigned to handle. ^[inferred]

A member of [[the_grung]] clan who planned to desert. When [[simone_tabarnack]] reported his intentions in 1494DR, the clan tortured and killed the gnome slave [[pell]] in reprisal. Haunted by Pell's death, Jean-Claude escaped east through [[the_midchain]], living rough off the land to avoid leaving a trail.

By Day −1 of 1495DR he has been running the Midchain for months. Simone's hunter unit has tracked him east and is one island behind him. He leaps aboard [[the_saltwright]] when he hears dogs behind him on a Midchain island — joining the rest of the party by accident. ^[extracted]

## What He's Running From

- [[simone_tabarnack]] — the clan member who reported him; now leads an elite hunter unit dedicated to running down deserters
- [[the_grung]] — his own clan, who killed Pell as institutional punishment
- His own guilt — the death of [[pell]] was a direct consequence of his plans
