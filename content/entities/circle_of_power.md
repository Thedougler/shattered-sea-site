# Circle of Power

## Overview

Circle of Power is a 5th-level Abjuration spell. For up to ten minutes (Concentration), the caster radiates a 30-foot Emanation. Every creature within the aura — caster and allies — has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. Furthermore, when an affected creature makes a saving throw against a magical effect that would deal half damage on a success, a successful save deals no damage instead of half.

Component is verbal only. Casting time is 1 action.

## Mechanics Notes

The combination of Advantage on saves and the half-to-zero rider makes Circle of Power exceptionally strong against area-effect spells (Fireball, Lightning Bolt, etc.) — the two effects stack to dramatically reduce expected damage output of AoE casters. At 30 feet the aura is large enough to cover most melee formations. The verbal-only component means it can be cast silently when magical silence is available or in armour without restriction.
