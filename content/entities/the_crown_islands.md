## Overview

Five large, mountainous islands forming the northern arc of [[the_verdant_scatter]]. Administered by [[the_dravosi_crown]]. The Dravosi regional fleet stages at Harwick.

## Settlements

- **[[port_tidefall]]** — on Calder's Tooth, the westernmost major island; sits at the western mouth of [[the_central_strait]]
- **[[calveno]]** — on Calven island; oldest commercial centre in the Scatter
- **[[the_warren]]** — beneath Calveno's Saltmarsh district; the only fixed Rattkin settlement in the Sea
- **[[the_high_eyrie]]** — not a settlement but a basalt sea stack off the northeastern Crown coast; watches [[the_drowned_maw]]

## Relationships

- [[the_verdant_scatter]] — northern arc of the primary archipelago
- [[the_dravosi_crown]] — governing colonial power
- [[the_central_strait]] — the corridor running along the Crown Islands' southern edge
- [[the_midchain]] — the ungoverned southern arc across the Strait
