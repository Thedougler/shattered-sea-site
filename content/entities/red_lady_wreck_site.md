## Overview

The wreck site of the [[red_lady]], sitting at the Drowned Maw's eastern edge where the continental shelf begins its descent toward the trench floor. The four other ships of [[fisks_fleet]] — *The Narrow*, *Heft*, *Fernen*, and *Loud Argument* — went down at the same time and lie nearby.

The [[pearl_of_souls]] sank with the Red Lady and still lies at the wreck site, now acting as a directed attractor drawing entities through the Maw fissure.

## Significance

The Red Lady wreck site is the deepest known location of direct campaign consequence. The Pearl resting here is the proximate cause of the Leviathan's breakthrough and the current crisis. All crew souls from five ships were drawn into the Pearl here.

The wreck also contains: Fleet Intelligence Room records (encoded contract logs, contractor correspondence, cipher keys), partial Maw bathymetry charts, and the locked stores of the flagship — all now at depth and underwater. ^[inferred]
