## Overview

The agricultural island of [[the_verdant_teeth]], two days south of [[kalowe]] in [[the_midchain]]. Green and Blue caste majority with a Purple-caste garrison. The largest slave population in the Verdant Teeth cluster — the [[botukuri]] clan operates the island's slave labour infrastructure.

Birth island of [[jean_claude_tabarnack]]. In 1495DR, [[simone_tabarnack]] leads Sorn's garrison and commands an elite hunter unit dedicated to running down deserters.

## The Botukuri Household

[[pell]], the gnome slave killed in reprisal for [[jean_claude_tabarnack]]'s desertion plans, was enslaved in the [[botukuri]] household on this island.
