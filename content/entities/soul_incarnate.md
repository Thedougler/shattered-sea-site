## Overview

A Soul Incarnate is a truly neutral lich of the monastic variety — an undead whose mummified body-phylactery is sealed somewhere safe, preserving the same consciousness indefinitely. Unlike the ambition-driven undead typical of lich lore, a Soul Incarnate seeks only continuity of purpose: to keep doing the thing they chose to do past the threshold of death. ^[inferred]

## Known Instances

> [!secret] Sentinels of the Eyrie — Late-Game Reveal
> The true head of [[sentinels_of_the_eyrie]] is a Soul Incarnate, phylactery sealed within [[the_high_eyrie]]'s basalt sea stack. The 200-year unbroken observation record is not institutional continuity — it is one consciousness watching the Maw for centuries. Living masters like [[master_kyzil]] have no awareness that the commands they follow originate from this source.
