## Overview

[[perrin_black_jaw]]'s ship — and before that, his grandfather's. The Vestra was [[nona_black_jaw]]'s late husband's vessel, passed down as a Black-Jaw family heirloom to Perrin. Destroyed by [[leviathan]] on Day −4, 1495DR, near [[keth_naar]]. Perrin survived the wreck — he believes his cloak saved him; it was actually [[auralis]], who forged a warlock pact with Perrin during the wreck without Perrin knowing.
