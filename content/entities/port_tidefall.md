## Overview

A major port settlement on Calder's Tooth, the westernmost significant island of [[the_crown_islands]]. Sits at the western mouth of [[the_central_strait]] — the entrance point for ships coming through [[the_galewall]] into the Scatter. Three days' sail from [[calveno]] on good wind.

Its position at the western mouth of the Strait makes it the logical first Dravosi checkpoint for traffic entering the Sea from the homelands. ^[inferred]
