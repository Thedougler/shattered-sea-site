## Overview

A hunter from [[keth_naar]]. His tribe reads the seas as "actively wrong" — before [[fisks_fleet]] passes (Day −7) the disturbance is already noticeable. After the fleet passes, Belmore is tasked as the tribe's strongest hunter to find the cause and learn why [[umberlee]] is furious.

On Day −4 he finds [[the_saltwright]] in Keth-Naar harbour and barters passage west with [[beaumont_sel]].

## Party Role

Belmore is listed as a party member (gravity_sources on the setting document names him). His mission — find the cause of Umberlee's fury — aligns directly with the Pearl theft and what's now happening at [[the_drowned_maw]]. ^[inferred]
