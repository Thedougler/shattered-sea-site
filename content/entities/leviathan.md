## Overview

An entity that forced through the planar fissure at [[the_drowned_maw]] on Day −5, 1495DR — three hours after the [[pearl_of_souls]] settled near the fissure and converted it into a directed attractor. The fissure had to be widened further to let it through; it forced this itself. ^[extracted]

[[auralis]], dormant for ~2000 years, woke specifically in response to Leviathan's crossing. He reads it as a parasitic invader in his realm. ^[extracted]

## Nature

The source describes it in lowercase before naming it — "something has already come through it" — suggesting Leviathan may be a label applied after the fact. ^[inferred] Whether it originates in the [[elemental_plane_of_water]] or passed through that plane from something deeper is unknown. ^[ambiguous] Below the fissure, "something older is the apex predator" — this may refer to Leviathan or to something it serves. ^[ambiguous]
