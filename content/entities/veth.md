## Overview

The largest island of [[the_verdant_teeth]]. Primary Gold-caste settlement, built on elevated canopy platforms not visible from the coast. The designated slave-purchase and trade beach sits on Veth's western coast — the only sanctioned point of contact between the Grung and outside traders.

## The West Beach

All legitimate trade with the Teeth happens here. Outside traders anchor offshore and wait. Blue-caste intermediaries come to the waterline and no further. The interior is not visible from the beach. The transaction is efficient and does not involve eye contact with anyone inland.

Rules: bring what they want, no extra people, payment through Blue-caste only. To address a Gold: you don't. You address a Blue.
