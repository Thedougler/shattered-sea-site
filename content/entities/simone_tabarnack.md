## Overview

A Grung — Jean-Claude's clan. In 1494DR she reported his desertion plans to the clan leadership, triggering the torture and death of the gnome slave [[pell]] in reprisal. In 1495DR, her purple-and-black coloration has transitioned to purple-and-gold, elevating her to warlord-tier status — just below pure gold in Grung hierarchy. ^[extracted]

She now leads [[sorn]]'s garrison and commands a small elite unit of purple Grung familiar with Jean-Claude's methods, dedicated to running down deserters.

## Motivation

The source does not specify why she reported Jean-Claude — whether from loyalty to the clan, personal animosity, or something else. ^[ambiguous] She is now actively hunting him with a specialist unit, suggesting this is personal as well as institutional. ^[inferred]

## Grung Color Hierarchy

Purple-and-gold is warlord-tier, one step below pure gold. The transition from purple-and-black to purple-and-gold in a year implies significant elevation — possibly as a reward for the information that exposed Jean-Claude. ^[inferred]
