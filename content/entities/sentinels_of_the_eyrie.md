## Overview

An Aarakocra Kensei monastic order based on the basalt sea stack known as [[the_high_eyrie]], off the northeastern Crown coast. Founded 895DR to counteract the [[waveservants]] of [[umberlee]] — a predatory tribute network extorting sailors for favorable weather.

Since 1295DR they have maintained an unbroken observation record of [[the_drowned_maw]] — the ledger series still running in 1495DR, now two centuries of continuous data. The most complete documentation of the Maw in the Scatter. The order predates all three colonial powers and has never submitted to any of them.

**Appearance:** Aarakocra in undyed grey linen, feathers salt-dusted from circuit work, each carrying a sealed observation ledger. Recognizable less by uniform than by the stillness they carry and the way their eyes track the horizon without appearing to.

## Doctrine: Objective Observation

The Sentinels practice **Objective Observation**: record what you see, exactly as you see it, without conclusion, interpretation, or editorializing. The record is the product. What it means is not their question. Sentinels speak in observation language — *"I recorded,"* not *"I saw."* They do not interpret what they have logged.

This is not passivity. The Sentinels will defend themselves, preserve the order, and help those in genuine need. What they will not do is let presence, alliance, or action compromise the clarity of what they can observe. A Sentinel who anchors over the Maw to study a wreck is no longer an objective observer of the Maw — they are a participant in it.

## History

- **895DR** — Founded at [[the_high_eyrie]] specifically to oppose [[waveservants]] extortion
- **1295DR** — Begin formal unbroken observation record of the Maw (600 years after founding)
- **1493DR** — [[crisdalynn_khinriss]] banished at age 14 for treating her assigned pilgrimage (mapping the Maw from inside a storm) as a joke. [[master_kyzil]] approves the decision.

## Structure

The order is small by design — a working observation post, not an army. Masters assign pilgrims and maintain the record. Sentinels conduct **circuits**: aerial observation runs over the Maw and adjacent waters, logged on return. The record is cumulative and institutional; no single Sentinel holds it all.

**Pilgrimage tradition:** The final stage of training. Each student is assigned a task by their master and sent out. They do not return until it is complete. Every Sentinel in the order's history has done this.

**Current state — thinning by design:** As of 1495DR, masters have dispersed Sentinels away from the peak as a precaution in response to worsening Maw conditions — increasing sinkings, wrecks, and abnormal weather. Those dispersed are not missing. They are where the order decided they should be.

## Secrets

> [!secret] The Head of the Order Is a Soul Incarnate — Late-Game Reveal
> The true head of the Sentinels of the Eyrie is a [[soul_incarnate]] — a truly neutral lich of the monk variety, their mummified body-phylactery sealed somewhere within the basalt sea stack beneath [[the_high_eyrie]]. They simply observe — the ultimate expression of the order's own doctrine, pushed past the threshold of death.
