## Overview

Sixty thousand square miles of reef and deep water scattered in two long arcs running east to west. The air changes before the first island is visible — warm and heavy, salt underlaid with something green, the smell of rainforest carried on the same wind that fills the sails. Limestone rises in clusters: pale cliffs and dark jungle canopy, reefs showing turquoise over white sand in the shallows. The northern arc runs high and mountainous; the southern arc is denser, lower, dozens of smaller islands packed close with narrow channels threading between them. Eastward, both arcs narrow and the cliffs grow higher, the water darker, the air colder.

Three colonial powers have been failing to fully govern the Sea for three centuries. The indigenous islanders predate them. Very old things live among them. ^[inferred]

## Geography

The Sea is bounded on four sides by natural and supernatural barriers:
- **West** — [[the_galewall]]: permanent storm belt, three-week crossing from the colonial homelands
- **East** — [[the_drowned_maw]]: deep-sea trench, 40–90 miles across, with a widening planar fissure at its floor
- **South** — [[the_doldrums]]: windless band, drifts 50–100 miles seasonally
- **East of the Maw** — [[the_outer_reach]]: open ocean, thin charts, no colonial presence

The primary landmass is [[the_verdant_scatter]], divided into the northern [[the_crown_islands]] and southern [[the_midchain]], converging at [[the_tail]] near the Maw.

## Central Conflict

A planar fissure beneath [[the_drowned_maw]] has been widening for centuries and connects to the [[elemental_plane_of_water]]. [[leviathan]] has already come through it. [[umberlee]]'s authority ends somewhere above the breach — below it, something older is the apex predator. The last guardian of that boundary, [[auralis]], has just woken up. ^[inferred]

## Navigation

Major ports measured in sailing days: [[port_tidefall]] to [[calveno]] is three days on good wind; Calveno to [[kalowe]] is four; Kalowe to [[the_tail]] is two. The colonial homelands lie three weeks through [[the_galewall]].
