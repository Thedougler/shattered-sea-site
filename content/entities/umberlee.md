## Overview

A sea deity whose authority ends somewhere above the planar breach at [[the_drowned_maw]]. Below that line, something older is the apex predator. The precise boundary is described as "somewhere above the breach" — even divine jurisdiction has an edge condition here. ^[inferred]

## Actions in 1495DR

When [[fisks_fleet]] stole the [[pearl_of_souls]] from her Blue Shrine on [[vel_orn]] (Day −6), Umberlee's retribution found them the following day over the Maw. She engulfed all five ships in fog, opened a rogue trench that drove the fleet 80 feet down, and closed the water overhead. As the hulls sank she pressed the souls of all five ship captains into [[delmar_fisk]]. All five crews were drawn into the Pearl itself.

The Waveservants failed to detect the theft — Umberlee's response was to the Pearl's absence, not to the theft in progress. ^[inferred]
