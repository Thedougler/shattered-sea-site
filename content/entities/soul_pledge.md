# Soul-Pledge

## Overview

A formal Antheri soul-binding tradition: a ritual that binds a consciousness to a purpose, an object, or a place — preserving it past ordinary death. [[auralis]] is the one confirmed surviving instance: bound to guard the Antheri creations at [[the_drowned_maw]], he survived the collapse of his civilization and ~2000 years of dormancy because his pledge kept him. His pre-ritual identity was erased by the binding.

The soul-pledge is not a one-off act. The deep city contains a **permanent ritual chamber** in Tier 3 of [[the_antheri_ruins]], sized and marked for repeated use, with symbolic language that recurs across all deeper tiers of the city. This indicates a formalized service tradition with an established ritual vocabulary — a practice performed multiple times across Antheri history.

## Architecture of the Ritual

The soul-pledge symbolic language integrates into the structural design of Tier 2 and Tier 3 and appears **load-bearing**, not decorative. This is unusual: the ritual vocabulary is not confined to the chamber itself but woven into the fabric of the mid and deep city. ^[inferred] The Antheri did not separate their binding tradition from their engineering tradition.

## Open Questions

What the other participants were bound to — and whether those bindings remain active — is not established. If bindings are still active:

- What they were bound to protect may still be operating.
- Or they may have **failed when the Antheri fell** with unknown downstream consequences. ^[ambiguous]

> [!secret] The soul-pledge chamber in Tier 3 may show floor and wall markers for distinct bindings. A party that reaches it and can read the Antheri script — or find a translation — could identify what else was bound, to what, and when.

## Conceptual Parallels

The soul-pledge shares conceptual space with [[astral_projection]], which separates consciousness from the physical body across planes, and with the [[soul_incarnate]] condition (see [[sentinels_of_the_eyrie]]). The soul-pledge is permanent and purpose-bound rather than temporary and voluntaristic. ^[inferred]
