## Overview

An engineer and salvager operating out of [[calveno]]. In 1488DR she salvaged a cache of functional [[the_antheri|Antheri]] components from the [[the_antheri_ruins|Shelfworks]] above the Antheri Ruins and successfully reverse-engineered several into working devices — a feat that had not been accomplished with Antheri technology before. ^[inferred]

She did not participate in the [[shelfworks_goldrush|Shelfworks Goldrush]] she inadvertently started. Instead she returned to Calveno and put her workshop into production.

## Work

- **Clockwork Owl Familiar** — her most visible creation from the 1488DR period; circulates widely enough that most people in the Sea have seen one
- **[[salvaged_antheri_plate]]** — in 1490DR, sold a fitted section of salvaged Antheri plate to [[beaumont_sel]], sized to cover a cannonball breach in his shell

## Significance

Her 1488DR reverse-engineering work is the proximate cause of the Shelfworks Goldrush — the wave of prospectors, scholars, and criminal contractors that descended on the Shelfworks. She is the only person known to have successfully reverse-engineered Antheri components into functional devices. ^[inferred]
