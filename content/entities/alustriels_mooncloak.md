# Alustriel's Mooncloak

## Overview

Alustriel's Mooncloak is a 5th-level Abjuration spell. For up to one minute (Concentration), moonlight fills a 20-foot Emanation centred on the caster, granting all creatures within it Half Cover and Resistance to Cold, Lightning, and Radiant damage.

The spell ends immediately when the caster activates one of two options:

- **Liberation** — When the caster fails a saving throw to avoid or end the Frightened, Grappled, or Restrained condition, they may use a Reaction to succeed instead.
- **Respite** — As a Magic action, the caster or an ally within the aura regains Hit Points equal to 4d10 plus the caster's spellcasting ability modifier.

Material component is a moonstone worth 50 GP or more (not consumed).

## Mechanics Notes

The spell is a one-shot: choosing either Liberation or Respite collapses the aura entirely. The decision point is therefore both tactical (when to spend the effect) and positional (keeping allies in the 20-ft emanation for Half Cover and resistances while it holds).

## Relationships

Both Alustriel's Mooncloak and [[circle_of_power]] are 5th-level Abjuration Concentration spells that create protective emanations centred on the caster, but they operate differently: Circle of Power keys off saving throws while Mooncloak provides passive resistances with a one-time burst trigger.
