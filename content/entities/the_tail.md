## Overview

Where both arcs of [[the_verdant_scatter]] narrow toward [[the_drowned_maw]]. High-cliffed, cold, with Maw-adjacent communities. The air gets colder and darker the further east you go. Kalowe to the Tail is two days' sail.

## Features

The [[the_antheri_ruins|Shelfworks]] — ruins of [[the_antheri]] — sit on the Maw's western rim at 60–200 feet depth, placing them at the Tail's eastern edge. ^[inferred]
