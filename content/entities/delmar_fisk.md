## Overview

Admiral of [[fisks_fleet]], commanding the flagship [[red_lady]]. Led the mission that successfully stole the [[pearl_of_souls]] from [[umberlee]]'s Blue Shrine on [[vel_orn]] on Day −6, 1495DR. The fleet was destroyed the following day by Umberlee's retribution over [[the_drowned_maw]].

[[crisdalynn_khinriss]] dived from the crow's nest, grabbed him, and flew them both clear of the sinking. He and Crisdalynn are the only two survivors of five ships and their crews.

## What He Carries

As the five hulls went down, [[umberlee]] pressed the souls of all five ship captains into Fisk. He is now carrying five dead captains' souls within him — a curse or binding whose mechanics and implications are not yet described in current sources. ^[ambiguous]

One of the five is named: [[rael_corven]], flag captain of the [[red_lady]].
