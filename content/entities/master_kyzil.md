## Overview

Senior master of the [[sentinels_of_the_eyrie]]. He staked his reputation on [[crisdalynn_khinriss]]'s readiness for her pilgrimage — map [[the_drowned_maw]] from inside a storm — then approved her banishment when she treated it as a joke.

Before she left, he gave her his **dreidel**, a Kensei teaching tool centered on balance and motion.

As Maw conditions worsened, the Eyrie's masters began dispersing Sentinels from the peak rather than concentrating the order. Kyzil was already away when the Red Lady went down. Using current and weather patterns, he deduced where she would likely wash ashore and came to [[port_tidefall]] to find her alive.

He has not yet told her that surviving the attempt is itself evidence of what the trial was meant to prove.

## Appearance

Aarakocra, tall and lean, built and colored like a barn owl. Pale, broad facial disc. Tawny-brown feathers fading into ash at crown and mantle. Plain sentinel robes with loose cuffs that occasionally reveal geometric forearm markings.

Two daggers orbit beside him without a hand on them. Stands with wings folded tight, posture straight, feet set with the still grip of a perched bird.

## Voice & Delivery

Questions first, answers second. Opens hard prompts with *"Who."* and lets silence do the work. Speaks as a master testing perception, not a commander issuing orders. Movement is minimal, precise, and often unsettlingly quiet.

> *"Who told you surviving does not count?"*
>
> *"Who were you when you entered the storm, and who came back?"*

## Active Problem & Goal

He does not know whether Crisdalynn will consider this nearly-fatal attempt at her pilgrimage complete. His immediate goal: hear her account of what she observed at the Maw, test her skills, and ask her whether she believes the pilgrimage is finished — without giving her the answer first.

## The Withheld Truth

He has not told her that surviving the attempt is itself evidence of what the trial was meant to prove. Whether and when he says this is open. ^[inferred]
