## Overview

A [[rattkin]] elder — matriarch of the Black-Jaw Run and Tangle elder of [[the_warren]]. The most senior voice in the Warren and a central node in [[the_passage]] network. Small, white-furred, sharp, and unhumbled by age.

The [[vestra]] was her late husband's ship. It passed to Perrin as a family heirloom. She does not yet know it was destroyed by the Maw anomaly. She has sent messages throughout the Passage asking Perrin to make contact.

## Appearance

Very small, even for a [[rattkin]]. White-furred from snout to tail-tip, with fur along her jaw and lower muzzle running dark brown — almost black — fading up toward pale cheeks. That's the name. Sharp pink-rimmed eyes set deep under a low brow. Old — thin fur around the ears, prominent knuckles — but she sits perfectly straight, hands folded, nothing hunched about her. Dark layered cloth, no adornment. Tail resting coiled at her feet.

## Voice & Manner

Feeds people first. Calls them thin. Sets her terms. Discusses anarchist theory and Dravosi arson in the same warm tone she uses for the weather.

If you don't enjoy her terms, there are Molotov cocktails for dessert.

**Roleplay concept:** Grandma mob boss. The warmth is real. So are the consequences.

## Active Problem

Something attacked one of her Run. Perrin Black-Jaw, specifically. She does not yet know that the *something* was [[leviathan]] and that the [[vestra]] is gone. When she finds out — that is a war footing.

## Position in the Passage

Her Tangle is the network's structural center; she is at the center of the Tangle. Within [[the_passage]], the Tangle appears to be the core operational unit anchored at [[the_warren]], making her effectively the network's most connected elder. ^[inferred]
