## Overview

The oldest commercial centre in the Scatter, on Calven island in [[the_crown_islands]]. Three days' sail from [[port_tidefall]], four days' sail from [[kalowe]]. Dravosi-administered.

Beneath Calveno's Saltmarsh district sits [[the_warren]] — the only fixed Rattkin settlement in the Sea, and the base of [[the_passage]] evasion network.

## History

- **1210DR** — [[the_tessarine_concordat]] arrives and establishes the **Seven Houses of Calveno**, beginning "the slow arithmetic of obligation" — a debt and obligation network that accumulates leverage over the city's commercial life
- **1240DR** — [[the_passage]] Rattkin network forms out of [[the_warren]] in direct response to both the Concordat and [[the_dravosi_crown]]
- **1488DR** — [[catarina_davirelli]] returns from the [[shelfworks_goldrush]] she triggered and sets up a workshop in Calveno; her [[clockwork_owl_familiar]] becomes ubiquitous across the Sea

## Relationships

- [[the_crown_islands]] — the island group Calveno belongs to
- [[the_warren]] — Rattkin settlement in the Saltmarsh district below
- [[port_tidefall]] — three days' sail west
- [[kalowe]] — four days' sail into the Midchain
- [[the_dravosi_crown]] — governing colonial power
