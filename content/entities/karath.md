## Overview

The raid-staging island of [[the_verdant_teeth]]. Purple-caste dominant. Outsiders who approach by water are met with dart fire without warning — no warning approach, no beach, no contact.

## The Expansion

Over the last two years Karath has expanded: more boats, more active raiders, ranges pushing into coastal Midchain settlements previously out of reach. The Island Council has received complaints and has not acted. Midchain locals are unaware of the scale.

The expansion pattern is organized, not opportunistic. Whatever is driving it has not yet shown itself. ^[inferred]

[[felix_aho]] is a Karath-origin defector — Green caste, left eight years ago after destroying several raiding groups on the way out. The Karath Gold know who did it. They have not found him.
