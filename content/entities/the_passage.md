## Overview

A Rattkin network operating out of [[the_warren]] beneath [[calveno]], formed in 1240DR to evade both [[the_tessarine_concordat]] and [[the_dravosi_crown]]. Founded thirty years after the Concordat's arrival — a direct response to the squeeze of two colonial powers on the same city. ^[inferred]

The name "the Passage" implies a network of routes, hidden transit, or information flow — consistent with Rattkin transient nature and the subterranean base in the Warren. ^[inferred]

## Structure

The Passage is organized around **Runs** (city-based community networks) and **Holds** (waypoints and safe-houses between cities). [[nona_black_jaw]]'s Tangle is the structural center, anchored at [[the_warren]]. She is the most senior voice in the Warren and the most connected Tangle elder known. ^[inferred]

Within Runs, variety roles are assigned by function: Black Rats work ports and elevated infrastructure; Brown Rats maintain Holds and extend tunnel networks; Fancy Rats handle long-term external relationships. A Fancy Rat cultivating a contact is not pulled for single-use jobs. ^[extracted]

The Calveno Run — anchored at [[the_warren]] — is the primary known Run. [[perrin_black_jaw]] is its most prominent current member in the field.
