## Overview

Five ships assembled under Admiral [[delmar_fisk]] by an unknown contractor via the Island Councils of [[the_midchain]]. Mission: steal the [[pearl_of_souls]] — an [[umberlee]] artifact — from the Blue Shrine on [[vel_orn]] in the [[the_sunken_crown]].

The fleet consisted of: *[[red_lady]]* (flagship, flag captain [[rael_corven]]), *The Narrow*, *Heft*, *Fernen*, *Loud Argument*. [[crisdalynn_khinriss]] was already serving aboard the Red Lady when the mission began.

## Aftermath

- [[delmar_fisk]] and [[crisdalynn_khinriss]] survive on driftwood
- All five ship crews drawn into the [[pearl_of_souls]]
- The Pearl settling near the Maw fissure converts it from passive elemental leakage to a directed attractor
- [[leviathan]] forces through 3 hours after the sinking
- [[beaumont_sel]] rescues Fisk and Crisdalynn on Day −3

## The Contractor

The commissioning entity is unknown. An unknown contractor engaged the Island Councils to assemble the fleet. Their identity and motivation for obtaining the Pearl of Souls is not established in current sources. ^[ambiguous]
