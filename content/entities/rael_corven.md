## Overview

Flag captain of the [[red_lady]], flagship of [[fisks_fleet]]. Commanded the ship under Admiral [[delmar_fisk]] during the mission to steal the [[pearl_of_souls]] from Umberlee's Blue Shrine on Vel Orn.

Died when all five ships sank over [[the_drowned_maw]] on Day −5, 1495DR. His soul — along with those of the four other fleet captains — was pressed into Fisk by [[umberlee]] to hold. ^[extracted]
