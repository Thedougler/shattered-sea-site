## Overview

A permanent storm belt marking the western boundary of [[the_shattered_sea]]. The three-week crossing through the Galewall separates the Scatter from the colonial homelands. Coming through it, the air changes before the first island is visible — warm and heavy, salt underlaid with something green. ^[extracted]

## Significance

The Galewall is the primary reason [[the_dravosi_crown]] and the other colonial powers have only partial control of the Sea. ^[inferred] The crossing is long enough that resupply and reinforcement from the homelands is a strategic liability. Ships and crews that make it through are effectively on their own for the duration of their time in the Scatter. ^[inferred]

## Relationships

- [[the_shattered_sea]] — the Galewall is its western boundary
