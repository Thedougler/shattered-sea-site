## Overview

An island in [[the_sunken_crown]]. Location of [[umberlee]]'s Blue Shrine, which held the [[pearl_of_souls]]. On Day −6 of 1495DR, [[delmar_fisk]], [[crisdalynn_khinriss]], and the crews of [[fisks_fleet]] successfully stole the Pearl without alerting the [[waveservants]]. The Pearl departed Vel Orn aboard the [[red_lady]].

## Significance

The Blue Shrine theft is the precipitating event for the entire campaign arc — it triggers Umberlee's retribution, sinks five ships, lands the Pearl on the Maw floor, and wakes [[leviathan]]. ^[inferred]
