# The Antheri

> *No sounding line has ever found the bottom of where they went. The ruins do not tell you what happened. They tell you it was fast.*

## Overview

The Antheri were an aquatic civilization centred on [[the_drowned_maw]]. Their city — known today as [[the_antheri_ruins|the Shelfworks]] — is a vertical trench-wall structure built into the Maw's western wall, descending from ~60 ft at the shelf edge toward unreached trench depth. They vanished c.-495DR; the ruins show no external attack and no mass casualties. Only [[auralis]] survived, bound by a soul-pledge that erased his pre-ritual memory. Their [[antheri_engineering]] still exceeds anything in active use in 1495DR, including the output of [[halythion]].

## Physical Form

Aquatic humanoids. No preserved visual record. Ruin evidence constrains the possibilities:

- Doorframes and furniture sized for a humanoid ~5–6 ft tall with narrower shoulder profile than a standard human
- Five-fingered hand-scale on tools and instruments
- Sleeping alcoves dimensioned for standard humanoid proportions
- **No pressure-management systems, no air pockets, no provision for surfacing** — they lived in open water without equipment

> [!secret] The ruins don't resolve exact physiology. Options consistent with the evidence: gilled humanoids with a secondary colour layer, deep-adapted humanoids with cartilaginous skeletal features, or something close to human shape with internal physiological differences invisible in architecture.

## Society

The Antheri record is architectural, not documentary.

**Upper shelf structures**: large-hall civic buildings, organized workshop spaces with fixed equipment stations — collective use, not private.

**Residential tier** (mid-depth): small units, densely arranged, stacked rows cut into the trench wall. No significant size variation — no obvious palaces, no subordinate housing. Either social equality, or hierarchy expressed outside housing.

**Deep structures**: singular, large, isolated, purpose-built — one facility per function for the entire civilization.

### The Soul-Pledge Tradition

The [[soul_pledge]] ritual that transformed [[auralis]] predates him. Tier 3 contains a permanent soul-pledge chamber sized and marked for repeated use, with symbolic language appearing across all deeper tiers. This is a formalized service tradition — not a one-off act. What other participants were bound to, and whether those bindings are still active, is not established.

> [!secret] The soul-pledge chamber may show evidence of multiple uses — markers on the floor or walls for different bindings. If some bindings remain active, what they were bound to protect may still be operating, or may have failed when the Antheri fell with unknown consequences.

### Relationship with the Maw

The city was deliberately built toward the planar boundary; deeper tiers become increasingly purpose-built rather than overflow. The surviving record does not establish whether the Antheri were studying, exploiting, or transforming their boundary interaction with the [[elemental_plane_of_water]]. [[halythion]] Remembrancers identify the Antheri as the civilization that operated closest to the Maw and report that their final deep-city project was never completed.

> [!secret] A party that reaches [[halythion]] can access Remembrancer accounts of the fall — the sea elves watched it happen from outside: accelerating activity in the deep city, then silence, then nothing coming up. It is the most complete external record of the Antheri's final period.

## Architecture and Materials

Antheri construction uses a stone composite no current materials expert has identified:

- Does not corrode. Does not foul. Biological growth does not adhere to it — salvaged pieces brought to the surface are as clean as the day they were set.
- Metalwork alloys unreproduce-able by current metallurgy.
- Mid-works machinery still functional after centuries without maintenance.
- **Salvaged Antheri material is valuable.** [[the_tessarine_concordat]] has standing purchase orders for intact structural pieces. [[cats_curios]] holds more Antheri instruments than any other private collection in the Scatter.

## Knowledge and Magic

| Capability | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Soul-binding** | Formal ritual architecture ([[soul_pledge]] chamber), stable symbolic vocabulary, repeated successful use. [[auralis]] is one documented instance of a broader practice. |
| **Elemental integration** | Deep-works construction incorporates elemental water into structural function rather than working around it. Mechanism not recoverable from exterior examination. |
| **Material science** | Composite materials with no mortal equivalent. Instruments more precise than current equivalents. Mid-works machinery functional after centuries unmaintained. |

Antheri planar understanding of the Maw exceeds current scholarship. Modern practice treats the Maw as a hazard; Antheri practice treated it as a study domain and engineering medium.

> [!secret] Intact Antheri research materials in Tier 2 could provide significant arcane advancement to a party that can read the script or find a translation. [[catarina_davirelli]] has reverse-engineered components from salvaged pieces and is the best current living non-Antheri expert on their material culture.

## The Fall

The physical record supports rapid civilizational failure, not gradual decline:

- No evidence of external attack on the shelf structures — the civic tier was **abandoned**, not destroyed
- No mass casualty event in the residential tier — sleeping alcoves and workspaces are **empty**, not occupied by remains
- Activity in the deep works was **ongoing at the time of the fall** — mid-process work present, tools set down rather than stored
- The fissure widened at some point — its current width is larger than what the city was originally built around

## What Current Civilizations Know

- **Sailors and civilians**: ruins are old and underwater; material is valuable; diving there is dangerous. Do not know the civilization's name, history, or its relationship to the Maw.
- **[[the_tessarine_concordat]] salvage operation**: knows the Antheri name; has basic records of shelf tier layout. Does not know the depth or extent of what lies below the shelf edge.
- **No colonial scholarly institution** has a working translation of the Antheri symbolic language.
