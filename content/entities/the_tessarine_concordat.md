## Overview

A power — colonial, mercantile, or both — that arrived in the Shattered Sea in 1210DR, fifteen years after [[the_dravosi_crown]] established its first colonial presence. The Concordat's method was not military but commercial: they formed the Seven Houses of [[calveno]] and "began the slow arithmetic of obligation." ^[extracted]

The phrase implies debt, obligation networks, and slow accumulation of leverage rather than direct governance. ^[inferred]

## History

- **1210DR** — Arrives in the Sea; forms the Seven Houses of [[calveno]]
- **1240DR** — [[the_passage]] Rattkin network forms out of [[the_warren]], explicitly to evade both the Concordat and the Dravosi Crown

## Seven Houses of Calveno

The Tessarine Concordat's primary structural creation. Nature, membership, and current power are unspecified in current sources beyond their founding context.
