## Overview

Five closely grouped islands in [[the_midchain]] interior, roughly two days south of [[kalowe]]. Dense rainforest interiors rising to elevated limestone ridges. Home territory of [[the_grung]]. All five appear on Midchain charts annotated with some version of *"do not approach interior"* or *"hostile inhabitants."*

> *The charts name them the Verdant Teeth and that is all they tell you. Five islands, packed close, limestone ridges lost under canopy so dense the treeline reads as a single wall from the water. No harbour on any of them. No smoke on the skyline. The only place to put a boat is the strip of pale sand on Veth's western coast, and even there you anchor offshore and wait. You do not approach the tree line. You leave before the light goes.*

## The Five Islands

**[[veth]]** — Largest island. Primary Gold-caste settlement, built on elevated canopy platforms not visible from the coast. The designated slave-purchase beach is on Veth's western coast — the only sanctioned point of contact between the Grung and outside traders.

**[[sorn]]** — Agricultural island. Largest slave population in the cluster. Green and Blue caste majority with a Purple-caste garrison. Home of the [[botukuri]] clan. [[jean_claude_tabarnack]]'s birth island; [[simone_tabarnack]] now commands its garrison.

**[[karath]]** — Raid staging island. Purple-caste dominant. Outsiders who approach by water are met with dart fire without warning. Karath has expanded — more boats, more active raiders — in a pattern that is organized, not opportunistic. Midchain locals are unaware of the scale.

**[[dreth]]** — Eastern island. Freshwater collection and plant cultivation. Green and Orange caste. Little military presence. One of the two islands a lost sailor might wander onto and survive.

**[[orak]]** — Eastern island, close to Dreth. Same function: freshwater and cultivation. Same caste composition and low military presence.

*([[aruhe]] — a deserted neighboring island east of Karath; not one of the five Teeth proper.)*

## Access & Trade

The only legitimate point of contact is **Veth's west beach**. Specific Midchain traders run the route regularly.

Rules of the beach:
- Bring what they want. Don't bring extra people.
- Payment goes through Blue-caste intermediaries. No seller comes inland.
- **Grung exports:** carved bone, resin objects, plant pigments, alchemical specimens
- **Grung imports:** preserved food, worked timber, plant materials for poison-crafting, salvaged metal and rope
- To address a Gold Grung: you don't. You address a Blue.

## Slave Economy

The Teeth have operated a slave economy for at least four generations — raiding coastal Midchain settlements, purchasing from outside traders at Veth's west beach, and working enslaved labour on Sorn's agricultural operations. The [[sentinels_of_the_eyrie]] observe and do not intervene. The Island Council receives complaints and files them.

[[pell]], the gnome slave killed in reprisal for [[jean_claude_tabarnack]]'s desertion, was enslaved on [[sorn]] in the [[botukuri]] household.

## Threat Escalation

Karath's expanded raiding capacity has pushed into settlements previously out of range. The Island Council has not acted. Midchain patience has a limit. Whatever is driving the escalation has not yet shown itself. ^[inferred]

## Jean-Claude's Arc

[[jean_claude_tabarnack]] was born on [[sorn]]. His desertion began the chain that killed [[pell]]. His terminal node is to return and end this. ^[inferred from source]
