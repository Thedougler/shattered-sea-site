## Overview

Green-caste Grung from Karath island in [[the_verdant_teeth]], now resident at [[port_tidefall]]. Defected eight years ago. Did not leave quietly — destroyed several Grung raiding groups on the way out using homemade explosives, deliberate and effective. The Karath Gold know who did it. They have not found him.

Since reaching the Midchain he has chosen a deliberately passive life — not out of fear, but because every Grung act of violence confirms what people already believe, and he is trying, one fish stall and one conversation at a time, to show them something different.

For eight years he has been building [[vaka]] — a loose community of Grung defectors and freed former slaves finding their footing outside [[the_verdant_teeth]].

**Appearance:** Lean green grung, three-and-a-half feet. Flat matte skin the colour of old leaves. Wide amber eyes, more expressive than most Grung allow themselves to be. Worn, overstuffed satchel across one shoulder. Moves quietly for his size.

## What He Knows

Félix was inside the Gold caste information system. He knows what lower castes don't:

- The Gold caste of each Verdant Tooth island operates independently and is frequently in conflict with the other islands' Golds.
- Each island's Gold controls what lower castes on *their own island* hear — so the clans appear unified from the inside.
- The unified front is a performance. One defector is an accident. Two is a pattern. The Grung can be divided.

This intelligence is directly relevant to [[jean_claude_tabarnack]]: the hunters pursuing him operate on Sorn's Gold's authority, not a coordinated inter-island command. ^[inferred]

## Voice & Manner

Soft, reedy voice. The Kermit pause before hard truths — *"Well... yeah. That's the thing."* Uses "we" almost exclusively; "I" slips out only when scared or caught off guard. States the thing plainly, then waits. If told no, he says "okay" and means it.

Goes completely still and quiet when someone he's protecting is threatened. The gentleness doesn't leave — it concentrates. He has made things explode before. He is choosing not to.

**Roleplay concept:** Kermit the Frog as a union organizer — gentle, earnest, perpetually slightly overwhelmed, but what he's building is real, it works, and he knows things that could change everything.

## Livelihood

Operates a fish stall called **Fare-Ia** at the Port Tidefall fish markets, selling **Ia-Miti**. This is his public face and how Grung defectors find him. The market is also how the expanding Grung raids are disrupting his carefully built quiet life.

## Active Problem

The expansion of Grung raids is making his peaceful, diligent life at the fish markets progressively less peaceful.
