# Cat's Curios

## Overview

A private collection — likely a shop or dealer — holding more intact Antheri instruments than any other private entity in the Verdant Scatter. [[catarina_davirelli]] has spent years working with pieces from this collection, reverse-engineering Antheri material science into the components that seeded the [[shelfworks_goldrush]].

Location in the Scatter is not specified in current sources.

## Significance

The collection represents the deepest accessible non-institutional archive of Antheri material culture. For a party seeking to understand Antheri engineering, find translation keys for Antheri script, or identify what specific instruments were used for, Cat's Curios is the most accessible starting point above the Shelfworks themselves. ^[inferred]
