## Overview

An artifact of [[umberlee]], held at the Blue Shrine on [[vel_orn]] in the [[the_sunken_crown]]. Guarded by [[waveservants]]. Stolen by [[fisks_fleet]] under Admiral [[delmar_fisk]] on Day −6, 1495DR without alerting the guards.

## What Happened to It

The Pearl never made it out of the Sea. On Day −5, Umberlee's retribution destroyed all five fleet ships over [[the_drowned_maw]]. The Pearl sank near the [[red_lady]] wreck at the Maw floor. When it settled near the planar fissure it continued functioning — drawing souls, accumulating divine energy — and in doing so converted the fissure from passive elemental leakage into a **directed attractor**, rapidly pulling entities through from the [[elemental_plane_of_water]].

Three hours later, [[leviathan]] forced through.

## What It Does

- **Draws souls** — all five fleet crews were drawn into the Pearl as the ships went down
- **Soul compression** — Umberlee pressed the souls of all five captains into [[delmar_fisk]] as the hulls sank
- **Fissure amplification** — its divine energy near the fissure actively accelerates planar crossings

## Current State

At the Maw floor. Actively functioning. The fissure is now a directed attractor rather than a passive leak. It is the single most dangerous object in the Sea's current state. ^[inferred]
