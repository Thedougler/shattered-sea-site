# The Antheri Ruins (The Shelfworks)

## Overview

The ruins of [[the_antheri]], commonly called the Shelfworks. Not a seafloor sprawl — a **vertical trench-wall structure** cut into the western wall of [[the_drowned_maw]], descending from ~60 ft at the shelf edge toward unknown trench depth. Each tier has distinct function, accessibility, and condition. [[auralis]] is the current guardian. Modern salvage crews have worked Tier 1 for decades; Tier 2 and below remain largely untouched.

## Materials

Antheri construction uses a stone composite no current expert has identified:

- Does not corrode. Does not foul. Biological growth does not adhere to it — salvaged pieces brought to the surface are as clean as the day they were set.
- Metalwork alloys are unreproduce-able by current metallurgy.
- Mid-works machinery still functional after centuries without maintenance.

Salvaged Antheri material is valuable. [[the_tessarine_concordat]] holds standing purchase orders for intact structural pieces and has basic records of the shelf tier layout. [[catarina_davirelli]] is the foremost living expert on Antheri material science.

## Tier 1 — The Shelf (60–200 ft)

Outermost tier; reachable with standard diving. Most portable components are stripped by long-term salvage. What remains: fixed machinery, integrated structures, some sealed storage, and symbolic wall reliefs preserving the civic face of the city. Recent damage is from **entity incursion** (post-Day −5, 1495DR), not original collapse — routine Tier 1 routes are now unsafe.

> [!mechanic] Standard dive access. Entity activity (post-Leviathan) has made previously safe routes hazardous. Sealed storage may still hold intact components.

## Tier 2 — The Mid-Works (200–500 ft)

Below safe diving range without magical assistance. Instrument reliability degrades at the boundary-zone edge, which limits access — and paradoxically preserves this tier better than Tier 1. What survives: **trench-wall housing clusters**, workshops with still-functioning fixed machinery, intact research spaces with instruments and Antheri script records. [[soul_pledge]] symbolic language is integrated into structural design at this depth and appears **load-bearing**, not decorative.

> [!secret] Intact Antheri research materials here could provide significant arcane advancement to a party that can read the script or find a translation. [[catarina_davirelli]] has reverse-engineered surface components but has never accessed Tier 2 directly.

> [!mechanic] Requires magical water breathing at minimum. Magical instrument degradation begins at this depth; navigation by dead reckoning only.

## Tier 3 — The Deep Works (500 ft and below)

Boundary-zone conditions dominate: active elemental diffusion, unstable pressure behavior, fluctuating visibility, distorted sound propagation, failed magical instruments, and inconsistent standard spell behavior. The Deep Works are **pristine** — completely undisturbed by modern salvage. Structures are intact, larger, and singular-purpose.

The [[soul_pledge]] chamber is in this tier and shows evidence of repeated formal use. The deepest known terminus structure is fissure-oriented; its function is not externally legible.

> [!mechanic] Reaching Tier 3 requires significant magical resource investment. Water breathing alone is insufficient — elemental diffusion damages mortal physiology. Ongoing CON saves against elemental pressure, spell failure chances, and exhaustion accumulate throughout.

> [!secret] The terminus structure at the fissure edge may be the answer to what the Antheri were actually doing. [[halythion]] Remembrancers know the sequence of the fall from outside but do not know what the Antheri were building toward.

## Current Situation (1495DR)

[[auralis]] has just woken (Day −5, 1495DR) after ~2000 years of dormancy. His guardianship instinct is active again; his relationship to the salvage operations and any party approaching the Shelfworks is undefined. The [[the_tessarine_concordat]] has an active salvage operation in Tier 1.
