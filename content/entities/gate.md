# Gate

## Overview

Gate is a 9th-level Conjuration spell (Concentration, up to 1 minute). The caster conjures a circular portal 5 to 20 feet in diameter, oriented in any direction, linking an unoccupied space within 60 feet to a precise location on a different plane of existence. The destination is visible through the portal. Movement through the portal's front side instantly transports travellers to the nearest unoccupied space on the target plane.

The portal has a distinct front and back on each plane; only movement through the front face allows transit.

**Creature summoning.** When casting, the caster may speak the true name of a specific creature on another plane (pseudonyms, titles, and nicknames do not work). The portal opens adjacent to that creature and transports it to the nearest unoccupied space on the caster's side. The caster gains no special power over the creature — it may leave, attack, or assist at the DM's discretion.

**Divine blocking.** Deities and planar rulers can prevent Gate portals from opening in their presence or within their domains.

## Campaign Relevance

The planar fissure at [[the_drowned_maw]] is a naturally occurring planar breach into the [[elemental_plane_of_water]] — it functions analogously to a permanent Gate that no mortal cast. [[Leviathan]] passed through that fissure, suggesting the boundary can be crossed by sufficiently powerful entities without magical assistance. ^[inferred] A caster attempting to use Gate to open a deliberate portal to the Elemental Plane of Water would be navigating the same planar geography, and the divine-blocking clause becomes relevant: any entity controlling the Maw could potentially prevent or redirect such a Gate. ^[inferred]

The true-name summoning clause is significant in a setting where entities like Leviathan and [[auralis]] operate at the boundary of planar influence: summoning by true name requires knowing the name, and true names of primordial planar entities are rarely in mortal possession. ^[inferred]

## Relationships

Gate and [[astral_projection]] are both 9th-level spells enabling planar transit, but through fundamentally different mechanisms — Gate creates a physical portal that allows bodily passage (or creature extraction), while Astral Projection routes consciousness through the Astral Plane leaving bodies behind.
