## Overview

A [[rattkin]] — specifically a **Fancy Rat** (hooded coloring) from the Calveno Run. Captain or crew of [[vestra]], which was destroyed by [[leviathan]] near [[keth_naar]] on Day −4, 1495DR. Perrin barely survived and limped onto the beach. He believes his cloak of the manta ray saved him — it was actually [[auralis]], who intervened in the wreck and in doing so forged a warlock pact with Perrin without Perrin knowing. ^[extracted]

He stowed away aboard [[the_saltwright]] in Keth-Naar harbour.

## The Unknowing Pact

Perrin is bonded to Auralis as a warlock patron without any awareness of it. Auralis woke three hours before the Vestra attack — saving Perrin was either deliberate strategic action (acquire a living agent) or reflexive guardian behavior in his domain. ^[inferred] Either way, Perrin's warlock powers, if he has any, derive from a 3,500-year-old Antheri guardian who has just woken from millennia of dormancy. ^[inferred]
