## Overview

The primary archipelago of [[the_shattered_sea]] and the primary setting of the campaign. Bounded by [[the_galewall]] to the west and [[the_drowned_maw]] to the east. Organized into two long arcs running east to west.

**Appearance:** Two arcs of islands running east to west — the northern arc broad-shouldered and mountainous, the southern arc dozens of low islands packed close — bounded to the west by a permanent storm belt on the horizon and narrowing to the east where the water turns dark and the charts stop making confident claims.

## Structure

| Region | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| [[the_crown_islands]] | Five large, mountainous islands. Northern arc. Dravosi-administered. |
| [[the_central_strait]] | 40–80 miles of deep water between the arcs. Dravosi inspection at western entrance. |
| [[the_midchain]] | Dozens of small to medium islands. Southern arc. Largely ungoverned. |
| [[the_tail]] | Eastern convergence. High-cliffed, cold, Maw-adjacent. |

Both arcs narrow and converge eastward at [[the_tail]], beyond which lies [[the_drowned_maw]].
