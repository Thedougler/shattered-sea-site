## Overview

A deep-sea trench 40–90 miles across at the eastern convergence of [[the_verdant_scatter]]. Compass drift begins within 5 miles of its edge. At its floor, a planar fissure connects to the [[elemental_plane_of_water]] — and that fissure has been widening for centuries.

## The Fissure

The breach at the Maw's floor is the campaign's central supernatural axis. [[umberlee]]'s authority ends somewhere above it — below, something older holds dominion. [[leviathan]] has already come through. [[auralis]], the last guardian of that boundary, has just woken up. ^[inferred]

The [[the_antheri_ruins|Shelfworks]] sit on the Maw's western rim at 60–200 feet depth — ruins of [[the_antheri]], positioned to watch or ward the trench. ^[inferred]

## Navigation Hazards

- Compass drift within 5 miles of the edge makes dead-reckoning unreliable ^[inferred]
- The deep runs sixty fathoms and does not come back up for a long time east of the Maw
- The [[the_outer_reach]] lies beyond — four days to nearest islands, no colonial presence

## Relationships

- [[the_high_eyrie]] — sea stack off the northeastern Crown coast that watches the Maw
- [[the_antheri_ruins]] — ancient ruins on the Maw's western rim
- [[auralis]] — waking guardian of the fissure boundary
- [[leviathan]] — entity already through the fissure
