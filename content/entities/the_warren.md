## Overview

The only fixed Rattkin settlement in [[the_shattered_sea]], located beneath [[calveno]]'s Saltmarsh district. The Warren's subterranean position and its designation as the *only* fixed Rattkin settlement implies Rattkin elsewhere in the Sea are transient or at-sea. ^[inferred]

In 1240DR, [[the_passage]] Rattkin evasion network formed out of the Warren to evade both [[the_tessarine_concordat]] and [[the_dravosi_crown]]. The Warren is the Passage's operational base. ^[extracted]
