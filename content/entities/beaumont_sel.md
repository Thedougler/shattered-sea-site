## Overview

Captain of the [[the_saltwright]]. A shelled creature — the source notes he commissioned Antheri plate "sized to cover the cannonball breach in his shell," indicating he has a natural shell that has taken damage. ^[extracted] His species is not specified in current sources. ^[ambiguous]

## History

- **1490DR** — Commissions and purchases a fitted section of salvaged [[salvaged_antheri_plate|Antheri plate]] from [[catarina_davirelli]]'s workshop in [[calveno]]
- **1495DR, Day −4** — [[belmore]] barters passage west aboard the Saltwright at [[keth_naar]]
- **1495DR, Day −3** — Crosses back east through [[the_drowned_maw]] and finds [[delmar_fisk]] and [[crisdalynn_khinriss]] on driftwood — the only two survivors of five ships. Pulls them aboard and gives them free passage west to [[port_tidefall]]. [[perrin_black_jaw]] is already hidden in the hold.

## Character Notes

Rescuing survivors and giving them free passage reads as either generosity or pragmatism — possibly both. ^[inferred] He is crossing the Maw in both directions during a period of extreme supernatural activity without apparent explanation of why.
