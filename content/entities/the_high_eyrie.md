## Overview

A basalt sea stack off the northeastern coast of [[the_crown_islands]]. Home to the [[sentinels_of_the_eyrie]], a Kensei monastic order founded here in 895DR specifically to counteract the [[waveservants]]. The Sentinels have maintained an unbroken observation record of [[the_drowned_maw]] since 1295DR — the ledger series still running in 1495DR. ^[extracted]

In 1493DR, [[crisdalynn_khinriss]] was banished from the Eyrie for treating her pilgrimage as a joke. Her assigned task — map the Drowned Maw from inside a storm — remains incomplete. ^[extracted]

## Significance

Two centuries of Maw observation data makes the High Eyrie's ledgers the most comprehensive record of the trench's behavior in existence. This record predates the Leviathan breach and the Pearl of Souls incident. ^[inferred]
