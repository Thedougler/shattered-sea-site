# Fire Bolt

## Overview

Fire Bolt is an Evocation cantrip. The caster hurls a mote of fire at a creature or object within 120 feet; the caster makes a ranged spell attack roll against the target. On a hit the target takes 1d10 Fire damage. A flammable object that is neither worn nor carried by a creature catches fire when hit.

Components are verbal and somatic. Casting time is 1 action; the effect is instantaneous.

## Relationships

Uses a ranged spell attack roll rather than a saving throw, making it reliable against low-AC targets but ineffective against creatures with high evasion. Longer range (120 ft) than [[acid_splash]] (60 ft), but targets a single creature only. One of the highest-damage single-target cantrips available.
