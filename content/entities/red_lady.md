## Overview

Flagship of [[fisks_fleet]]. A two-masted brigantine, 96 feet on the waterline, privateer-rigged. Hull stained deep red from waterline to rail — not painted, stained into the wood, uniform and deliberate. Figurehead: a cloaked woman in pale wood, eyes forward, one hand open at her side.

Built for extended independent operations: fast enough to disengage, armed enough to press, fitted for months without port resupply. She was in prime condition when she sank — no deferred maintenance, fully crewed and armed.

Flag captain: [[rael_corven]]. Admiral [[delmar_fisk]] commanded from her decks.

**Current location:** Drowned Maw — Shelfworks eastern edge, shelf descent. See [[red_lady_wreck_site]].

**Raised form:** Umberlee has raised her as a spectral pressure instrument — see [[dead_lady]].

## Deck Layout

**Weather Deck** — Mostly open. Raised afterdeck holds wheel, compass, speaking grates, and the Signal Station. Stern cabin block contains Captain's Quarters (starboard) and Fleet Intelligence Room (center-aft). Forward: mainmast working space, hatch gratings, pumps. Bow: anchor gear, forward swivel mount. Stern rail: aft swivel.

**Cannon Deck** — The ship's loud, crowded heart. Seven cannon ports per side. Central passage kept clear. Navigator's Station at the stern in a chart room. Officers' mess and first mate's berth nearby. Midships: main hatch, rope lockers, sail repair, tool chests. Forward: crew hammocks, mess table, bosun's locker, heads screened near the bow.

**Lower Belowdecks** — Surgeon's Berth at center with braced operating table and recovery space. Around it: lamp room, sailcloth and rope lockers, water casks, salted food stores, carpenter's stores, marines' ready room. Passage kept clear from ladders to surgeon.

**Hold** — Powder magazine and Weapons Locker at the rear, behind locked doors, strict lantern rules. Cook's Galley at center: brick-lined, fresh-water cistern, dry stores. Rest: ballast, cargo lashings, spare timber, chain, tar, bilge access.

## The Wreck

[[umberlee]]'s retribution came as fog first — so thick the water was invisible from deck, surrounding all five ships simultaneously. A rogue trench opened in the fleet's path and plunged all five ships 80 feet down into a valley of water. The walls closed in and swallowed the fleet whole.

[[crisdalynn_khinriss]] dove from the crow's nest, grabbed [[delmar_fisk]], and flew straight up — she had only risen 50 feet when the water closed in; momentum and buoyancy carried them both to the surface. They heard [[umberlee]]'s laughter on the wind, then her rage, as the ships went under. The Red Lady went down with the [[pearl_of_souls]] still aboard.

All hands lost. Souls collected by the Pearl at the wreck site. Five captains' souls — including [[rael_corven]] — were pressed into [[delmar_fisk]] to hold for later. The other four ships (*The Narrow*, *Heft*, *Fernen*, *Loud Argument*) lie nearby.
