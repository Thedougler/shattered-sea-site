## Overview

The Grung clan controlling [[the_verdant_teeth]], five islands in [[the_midchain]] two days south of [[kalowe]]. They hold slaves — [[pell]] is described as a gnome slave.

## Color Hierarchy

Skin color is determined at birth and defines social caste for life. Full caste table from the [[grung]] species entry:

| Color | Role |
|---|---|
| **Gold** | Clan leaders, spiritual authority |
| **Red** | Scholars, memory-keepers, poison-crafters |
| **Orange** | Artisans, builders, weapon-makers |
| **Purple** | Warriors, hunters, law-keepers |
| **Blue** | Scouts, messengers, handlers of slaves |
| **Green** | Labourers (lowest freeborn caste) |

Sub-tiers within Purple noted from campaign sources:
- **Purple-and-black** — high-ranking warrior tier ([[simone_tabarnack]]'s former coloration)
- **Purple-and-gold** — warlord tier (Simone's current coloration, "just below pure gold")
- **Pure gold** — apex

## Gold Caste — Internal Structure (Intelligence from Félix Aho)

The unified Grung front is a performance. Each Verdant Tooth island's Gold caste operates independently and is frequently in conflict with the Golds of other islands. Each island's Gold controls what lower castes on *their own island* hear — so the factions appear unified from the inside while actively disagreeing with each other at the top.

The practical consequence: the hunter unit pursuing [[jean_claude_tabarnack]] operates on Sorn's Gold's authority, not a coordinated inter-island command. One defector is an accident. Two is a pattern. The Grung can be divided. ^[inferred]

Source: [[felix_aho]] — green-caste Karath defector with eight years outside the system; was inside the Gold information network before he left.

## 1494–1495DR Events

In 1494DR, [[jean_claude_tabarnack]] planned to desert. [[simone_tabarnack]] reported him; the clan tortured and killed the gnome slave [[pell]] in reprisal. Jean-Claude escaped east. In 1495DR Simone has been elevated to warlord-tier and leads [[sorn]]'s garrison with a specialist hunter unit. ^[extracted]
