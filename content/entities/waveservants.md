## Overview

A predatory tribute network operating in [[umberlee]]'s name. Extorts sailors for favorable weather — pay or your voyage suffers. Active in the Sea since at least 895DR, when the [[sentinels_of_the_eyrie]] were founded specifically to counteract them. ^[extracted]

In 1495DR they guard [[umberlee]]'s Blue Shrine on [[vel_orn]], which held the [[pearl_of_souls]]. They were not alerted when [[fisks_fleet]] stole it on Day −6 — a significant operational failure. ^[extracted]

## History

- **895DR** — [[sentinels_of_the_eyrie]] founded at [[the_high_eyrie]] to oppose Waveservant extortion
- **1495DR, Day −6** — Guarding the Blue Shrine on Vel Orn when Fisk's Fleet stole the Pearl without detection
