## Overview

An island in [[the_sunken_crown]]. [[belmore]]'s home island — his tribe has been watching the seas turn wrong for days before [[fisks_fleet]] passes on Day −7.

On Day −4, [[leviathan]] destroys [[vestra]] and shipwrecks [[perrin_black_jaw]] on the beach here. Perrin survives and stows away aboard [[the_saltwright]] in the harbour. [[beaumont_sel]] is also in harbour; [[belmore]] barters passage west with him.
