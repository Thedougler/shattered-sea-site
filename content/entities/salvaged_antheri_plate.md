## Overview

A piece of custom-fitted salvaged [[antheri_engineering|Antheri plate]] purchased by [[beaumont_sel]] from [[catarina_davirelli]]'s workshop in [[calveno]] in 1490DR. Specifically sized to cover a cannonball breach in his shell — implying both that Beaumont is a shelled creature and that [[catarina_davirelli|Catarina]] has enough understanding of Antheri materials to cut and fit them to non-standard specs. ^[inferred]

## Significance

It demonstrates that Antheri materials can be adapted for individual use, not merely replicated as devices. The corrosion-proof, composite nature of Antheri stone makes this a highly durable repair. ^[inferred]
