## Overview

Dozens of small to medium islands packed close, with narrow channels threading between them. Forms the southern arc of [[the_verdant_scatter]]. Largely ungoverned — the source notes the Midchain "flies whatever its communities feel like flying, which is frequently nothing at all." ^[extracted]

## Settlements & Locations

- **[[kalowe]]** — free port, Tier 2 dry dock, built across three reef islands
- **[[halythion]]** — sea elf city inside the Shearwall Reef, invisible from the surface
- **[[the_verdant_teeth]]** — five islands of Grung territory, two days south of Kalowe

## Boundaries

The southern edge of the Midchain borders [[the_doldrums]], the windless band that drifts 50–100 miles seasonally.

## Relationships

- [[the_verdant_scatter]] — southern arc of the primary archipelago
- [[the_central_strait]] — corridor along the Midchain's northern edge
- [[the_dravosi_crown]] — colonial power whose authority largely stops at the Strait ^[inferred]
- [[the_doldrums]] — southern boundary
