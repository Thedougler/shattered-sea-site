## Overview

Rattkin get into places they are not supposed to be. A city's walls keep out armies — they don't keep out Rattkin. A locked cargo hold is a delay, not a barrier. A ship's bilge, a drainage pipe, a gap between walls that a human would dismiss as impassable: to a Rattkin, these are hallways. Combined with senses calibrated for threat detection and a survival instinct sharpened across generations of being hunted, Rattkin are as close to uncatchable as a creature comes.

In the Shattered Sea, Rattkin are the oldest continuous presence in every major port. The [[the_passage]] is the canonical Rattkin faction — the traveling network of Runs and Holds spanning every sea in the Scatter.

**Creature Type:** Small Humanoid · **Speed:** 30 ft · **Life Span:** ~25 years

## Species Traits

**Boneless.** Hinged ribcage, absent collarbone, elastic connective tissue. Can move through spaces as if Tiny. Speed halved while doing so. Advantage on checks to escape a Grapple or Restrained condition.

**Keen Senses.** Advantage on Perception checks relying on hearing or smell. Can detect invisible or hidden creatures within 5 feet via scent alone (not exact location).

**Scurry.** When taking the Disengage action, can move through spaces occupied by Large or smaller creatures without provoking Opportunity Attacks. Must end turn in an unoccupied space.

**Survivor.** Centuries of targeted poisoning and pest-eradication campaigns produced a bloodline that doesn't die easy. Advantage on saves against Poisoned condition and disease. Resistance to Poison damage.

**Swimmer.** Swim Speed equal to Speed.

**Pack Tactics.** When a visible creature within 5 feet is hit by another creature, can use a Reaction to make one weapon attack against that creature. Uses per rest equal to Proficiency Bonus; recharges on Short or Long Rest.

## Three Varieties

### Black Rat

*Slender, large-eared, dark brown or black fur, pointed nose. Smallest. Most common in warm port cities.*

Black Rats think in three dimensions. They default to elevation — mast tops, rooflines, rigging — and their bodies are built for it. Within [[the_passage]], Black Rats work ports, ships, and elevated infrastructure, and are the first to vanish when a Run is compromised.

- **Climber.** Climb Speed equal to Speed.
- **Roof Runner.** Long Jump 20 ft, High Jump 10 ft — with or without a running start.

### Brown Rat

*Stocky, coarse brown or grey fur, blunt nose, small ears, tail shorter than body. Largest variety.*

Brown Rats go below. Sewers, bilge lines, drainage networks, flooded tunnels. Within [[the_passage]], Brown Rats maintain the Holds, extend tunnel networks, and map every new harbour construction before the Passage uses it. The Warren's tunneling is Brown Rat work.

- **Darkvision.** 60 feet.
- **Burrower.** Burrow Speed 15 ft through earth, sand, and loose material. Cannot burrow through solid rock or worked stone.

### Fancy Rat

*Variable coloring — spotted, hooded, solid, roan. Softer features. Docile temperament. Most readable to non-Rattkin.*

Fancy Rats are the community's go-betweens. Where Black and Brown Rats rely on physical access, Fancy Rats rely on social access — they read people the way Black Rats read rooflines: as terrain with paths through it. The Passage reserves Fancy Rats for long-term outside relationships. They are not sent on single-use jobs. A Fancy Rat who has cultivated a contact inside a Tessarine factor office for three years is not sent to retrieve stolen cargo.

- **Adaptable.** Proficiency in two skills and one language of choice.
- **Silver Tongue.** On a failed Persuasion or Deception check, can use a Reaction to reroll. Uses equal to Proficiency Bonus; recharges on Long Rest.
