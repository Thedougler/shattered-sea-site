## Overview

The last surviving member of [[the_antheri]], born c. -2000DR during their civilization's height. His pre-pledge identity is lost. Bound by soul-pledge to guard the Antheri creations at [[the_drowned_maw]]. When the Antheri vanished in -495DR — and the planar breach widened beyond what the city was originally built around — Auralis survived as the sole living Antheri and has remained dormant since.

He woke on Day −5, 1495DR, approximately three hours after [[fisks_fleet]] sank over the Maw — triggered by [[leviathan]] forcing through the fissure. He reads Leviathan as a parasitic invader in his realm and has begun moving for the first time since the Antheri fell. ^[extracted]

## The Soul-Pledge

Auralis is bound by a [[soul_pledge]] to guard the Antheri creations — [[the_antheri_ruins]] and presumably the fissure boundary itself. The pledge survived the Antheri's disappearance and his ~2000-year dormancy. His waking is a direct response to a threat to what he's pledged to guard. ^[inferred]

The soul-pledge is a formalized Antheri tradition, not a unique act for Auralis. The Tier 3 chamber shows repeated formal use; symbolic language from the ritual is load-bearing in Tier 2 architecture. His is one documented instance of a broader practice — what other bindings exist and whether they remain active is unknown. The binding erased his pre-ritual identity entirely.

## The Warlock Pact

Perrin Black-Jaw believes his cloak of the manta ray saved him when [[vestra]] was destroyed. It was actually Auralis intervening — and in doing so, forging a warlock pact with Perrin without Perrin's awareness. Perrin is currently an unknowing warlock patron-bonded to a 3,500-year-old guardian who just woke up. ^[inferred]
