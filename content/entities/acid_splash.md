# Acid Splash

## Overview

Acid Splash is an Evocation cantrip. The caster conjures an acidic bubble at a point within 60 feet that detonates in a 5-foot-radius Sphere on arrival. Every creature in that area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 Acid damage; on a success, they take none.

Components are verbal and somatic. Casting time is 1 action; the effect is instantaneous.

Targets an area rather than a single creature, distinguishing it from attack-roll cantrips like [[fire_bolt]]. The Dexterity save mechanic makes it effective against heavily armoured targets who lack evasion.
