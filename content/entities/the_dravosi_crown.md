## Overview

One of three colonial powers contesting [[the_shattered_sea]] for three centuries — and the most visible in the Scatter. The Dravosi Crown administers [[the_crown_islands]] and the northern arc; its flag flies at every major anchorage there. Regional fleet stages at Harwick. Dravosi inspection controls the western entrance to [[the_central_strait]].

The source mentions three colonial powers total but names only the Dravosi Crown explicitly. ^[ambiguous]

## Control & Infrastructure

- Administers the five islands of [[the_crown_islands]]
- Controls [[port_tidefall]] (western Strait mouth) and [[calveno]] (oldest commercial center)
- Maintains the only formal ship inspection point in the Scatter (Central Strait western entrance)
- Regional naval fleet staged at Harwick (location: Crown Islands, exact island unspecified)

## Limits of Power

Despite three centuries, governance remains partial. [[the_midchain]] flies "whatever its communities feel like flying, which is frequently nothing at all." The colonial homelands are three weeks through [[the_galewall]], making sustained military reinforcement expensive. ^[inferred]

## Relationships

- [[the_crown_islands]] — primary administrative territory
- [[the_central_strait]] — controls western entrance
- [[the_galewall]] — three-week crossing to colonial homelands
- [[the_verdant_scatter]] — only the northern arc is firmly held
