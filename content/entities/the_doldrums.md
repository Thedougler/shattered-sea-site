## Overview

A windless band below [[the_midchain]]'s southern edge. Drifts 50–100 miles seasonally, making its exact position unpredictable. Sail-only ships caught in it can sit for days without wind to move. ^[extracted]

## Navigation Hazard

The seasonal drift means the Doldrums' southern boundary of safe sailing is not fixed. ^[inferred] Ships heading south from the Midchain need current intelligence on where the calm band sits. ^[inferred]
