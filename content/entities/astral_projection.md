# Astral Projection

## Overview

Astral Projection is a 9th-level Necromancy spell with a 1-hour casting time and a duration of "until dispelled." The caster and up to eight willing creatures within 10 feet project astral forms into the Astral Plane, leaving physical bodies behind in suspended animation — unconscious, not requiring food or air, and not ageing.

An astral form mirrors the projecting creature's statistics, appearance, and possessions exactly, with one addition: a silvery cord trailing from between the shoulder blades. The cord fades from view after 1 foot. Only specific effects that explicitly state so can sever the cord; if cut, both the body and the astral form die.

When an astral form leaves the Astral Plane for another plane, the physical body travels along the silver cord and re-manifests on that new plane. Damage to the astral form and damage to the physical body are entirely independent — effects on one do not transfer to the other. If either drops to 0 Hit Points, the spell ends for that creature.

## Mechanics Notes

The consumed material cost — a jacinth worth 1,000 GP and a silver bar worth 100 GP per target — makes large-group projections expensive. The spell ends for all targets if the caster takes a Magic action to dismiss it, giving the caster control over group recall.

## Relationships

The silver cord mechanic — a tether binding consciousness to body across planes — shares conceptual ground with [[soul_incarnate]], whose existence as preserved post-death consciousness explores what persists when the physical vessel is removed. ^[inferred] Both represent the question of whether identity and awareness are separable from the body.

Planar travel via Astral Projection connects to the planar geography of the Shattered Sea: the fissure at [[the_drowned_maw]] opens into the [[elemental_plane_of_water]] rather than the Astral Plane, but the two mechanics illuminate how different planes can be accessed. Compare [[gate]], which creates a direct physical portal rather than a projected-consciousness route.
