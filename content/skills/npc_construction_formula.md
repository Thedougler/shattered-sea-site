## Formula

> **Public image + real competence + one humiliating need + one petty fixation**

Every NPC in [[the_shattered_sea]] has all four components. A flat, professional NPC without a need and fixation is a missed opportunity.

## Examples from Source

| Public Image | Real Competence | Humiliating Need | Petty Fixation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Naval legend | Brilliant tactician | Needs royal approval | Obsessed with seeming younger than rival |
| Scandalous aristocrat | Master of boarding warfare | Fears tenderness | Ruins diplomacy if wardrobe is insulted |
| Aloof intellectual | Terrifying storm-caster | Needs recognition | Starts feuds over titles and terminology |

## Application

- The need and fixation are what make NPCs *disasters* — the competence is what makes them *dangerous*.
- The petty fixation should be something that can plausibly derail an otherwise functional scene.
- The humiliating need is what the NPC will never admit but will consistently act on.

See [[shattered_sea_tone_guide]] for the full tone context and [[scene_construction_rule]] for how NPCs plug into scene construction.
