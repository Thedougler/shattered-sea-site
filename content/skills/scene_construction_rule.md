## The Five Components

Every major scene in [[the_shattered_sea]] combines:

1. **A real external threat** — battle, storm, monster, curse, betrayal
2. **A petty internal conflict** — insult, jealousy, rank dispute, old fling, unpaid tab, grammar, credit, décor
3. **Stylish presentation** — beauty, wealth, elegance, rot
4. **Fast verbal escalation** — interrupting, misunderstanding, dominance, callback
5. **Consequences that remain real** — injury, debt, scandal, ship damage, political fallout

*Guideline, not a requirement for every encounter.*

## Why It Works

The formula captures the campaign's core tension: the external threat is serious, the internal conflict is absurd, the presentation is gorgeous, and the consequences are permanent regardless of how the conflict started. ^[inferred]

## Application

- The petty conflict (component 2) should feel disproportionate to the external threat (component 1).
- Stylish presentation (component 3) is what distinguishes this campaign's aesthetics — never let a scene feel drab even when it's grim.
- Verbal escalation (component 4) is where [[npc_construction_formula]] petty fixations and humiliating needs activate.
- Real consequences (component 5) prevent the comedy from deflating the stakes.
