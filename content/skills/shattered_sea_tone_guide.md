> **Agent-facing reference.** Use when generating content, NPCs, scenes, or dialogue for [[the_shattered_sea]] campaign.

## Core Equation

**High competence trapped inside low emotional maturity.**

Real danger, glamour, hierarchy, and momentum — populated by stylish, capable people who let ego, vanity, lust, boredom, resentment, and old grudges complicate their best efforts.

**One-sentence tone:** Swashbuckling sophistication often complicated by pettiness.

## Six Pillars

**1. Real stakes + childish dysfunction**
The setting plays straight. The people are disasters. Storms, monsters, betrayal, and sorcery are deadly and consequential. The immediate complications are stupidly personal.
Pattern: *epic consequences, petty triggers.*

**2. Competence without adulthood**
Characters are dangerous experts and personal disasters. Brilliant captain, terrible impulse control. Feared corsair, desperate for approval. Elegant diplomat, cannot let an insult go. Genius mage, jealous and impossible. Lethal officer, emotionally fragile.

**3. Stylish surface, rotten interior**
Lacquered warships and gilt figureheads. Velvet officers with enchanted pistols. Perfumed courts built on piracy or exploitation. Fine wine, blood on cuffs, smiling hatred.

**4. Banter as combat**
Dialogue is social violence: interruption, one-upmanship, flirtation as dominance, sarcasm masking hurt, precision insults, status testing, old slights never forgotten. Not random quips — weaponized wit.

**5. Mundane pettiness in lethal situations**
A boarding action pauses to argue about bad gunpowder. A kraken attack becomes a paperwork problem. A treaty fails over seating and décor. A villain summons a leviathan over a personal slight. A patron cares more about mahogany damage than the ghost ship.

**6. Cynicism without emptiness**
Institutions are dysfunctional clown cars run by egomaniacs. Yet people still have messy loyalty, attraction, history, and occasional genuine care. Mock heroism and competence — but never so hard that nothing matters.

## Tone Boundaries

| Keep | Avoid |
|------|-------|
| Glamour, danger, decadence, speed | Broad clown comedy or pure parody |
| Sharp dialogue and recurring grudges | Meme humor or nonstop one-liners |
| Emotional damage played lightly | Making the world itself too silly |
| Stylish people behaving badly | Every NPC sarcastic in the same voice |
| Real consequences | Removing consequences |

## World Feel

Everyone is tired. Everyone is performing. Everyone wants something. Everyone is one drink, one insult, or one bad memory away from making it worse — yet many still care, however dysfunctionally.

## Agent Application

- Default NPC dialogue register: status-testing, mildly competitive, guarded about real needs.
- Default scene energy: competent professionals sabotaged by interpersonal baggage.
- Default world texture: beautiful, expensive, slightly rotten.
- When generating NPCs, apply [[npc_construction_formula]]. A flat, professional NPC is a missed opportunity.
- When generating scenes, use [[scene_construction_rule]] — layer a petty conflict onto the tactical situation.
- Comedy comes from character flaws, not world silliness. The sea is deadly. The monsters are real. The people are disasters.
