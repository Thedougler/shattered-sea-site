> **Current year: 1495 DR**

## The Age of the Antheri

- -2000DR The [[the_antheri]] civilization at its height; city built into the western wall of [[the_drowned_maw]]; [[auralis]] born.
- -495DR Antheri vanish (no external attack); planar breach widens; [[auralis]] survives as sole Antheri; begins dormancy.

## The Shattered Sea Takes Shape

- 895DR The [[sentinels_of_the_eyrie]] founded at [[the_high_eyrie]] to counter [[waveservants]] extortion.
- 1195DR [[the_dravosi_crown]] establishes first colonial presence; claims [[the_crown_islands]]; forms [[port_tidefall]].
- 1210DR [[the_tessarine_concordat]] arrives; forms Seven Houses of [[calveno]].
- 1240DR [[the_passage]] Rattkin network forms out of [[the_warren]] to evade Concordat and Dravosi Crown.
- 1295DR Sentinels begin unbroken Maw observation record (still running in 1495DR).

## The Years Before

- 1488DR [[catarina_davirelli]] reverse-engineers Antheri components from [[the_antheri_ruins\|the Shelfworks]]; triggers [[shelfworks_goldrush]].
- 1490DR [[beaumont_sel]] commissions [[salvaged_antheri_plate]] from Catarina.
- 1493DR [[crisdalynn_khinriss]] banished from High Eyrie at age 14; meets [[delmar_fisk]]; ships aboard [[red_lady]].
- 1494DR [[jean_claude_tabarnack]] plans desertion; [[simone_tabarnack]] reports him; [[pell]] tortured to death; Jean-Claude escapes east.
- 1495DR [[simone_tabarnack]] elevated to warlord-tier; leads [[sorn]] garrison and hunter unit.

## The Final Days (1495 DR)

- Day −35 In the Midchain an Unknown contractor assembles [[fisks_fleet]] under Admiral [[delmar_fisk]].
- Day −7 [[keth_naar]] Fleet passes; [[belmore]]'s tribe notices seas turning wrong.
- Day −6 [[vel_orn]] Fleet steals [[pearl_of_souls]] from [[umberlee]]'s Blue Shrine without alerting [[waveservants]].
- Day −5 [[the_drowned_maw]] Umberlee's retribution destroys all five ships; Pearl sinks to Maw floor; five captains' souls pressed into Fisk; crews drawn into Pearl.
- Day −5 +3hr Maw fissure Pearl amplifies fissure; [[leviathan]] forces through; [[auralis]] wakes.
- Day −4 [[keth_naar]] Leviathan destroys [[vestra]]; [[perrin_black_jaw]] shipwrecked; [[auralis]] forges unknowing warlock pact with Perrin; Belmore boards [[the_saltwright]].
- Day −3 Maw / Saltwright [[beaumont_sel]] finds Fisk and Crisdalynn on driftwood; pulls them aboard; free passage to [[port_tidefall]].
- Day −1 Midchain [[jean_claude_tabarnack]] one island ahead of Simone's hunters.
