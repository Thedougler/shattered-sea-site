## Overview

While a creature has the **Blinded** condition, it experiences two effects:

- **Can't See.** The creature can't see and automatically fails any ability check that requires sight.
- **Attacks Affected.** Attack rolls against the Blinded creature have Advantage. Attack rolls the Blinded creature makes have Disadvantage.

## Tactical Implications

Blinded is a strong debuff — it creates a double-edged attack disadvantage asymmetry. Enemies gain Advantage attacking a Blinded target while the target simultaneously suffers Disadvantage on its own attacks. Combined with automatic failure on sight-dependent checks, it effectively removes a creature from ranged or perception-gated actions. ^[inferred]
