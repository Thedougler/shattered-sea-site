## Overview

The Bard is a charisma-based full caster (d8 hit die) with access to full spell slots and prepared spells scaling from 4 at L1 to 22 at L20. Bards cast spells as full casters (same progression as Wizards) using Charisma as their spellcasting ability. From L10 onward, they can prepare spells from the Bard, Cleric, Druid, and Wizard spell lists (Magical Secrets).

## Core Mechanics

**Primary Ability:** Charisma
**Hit Die:** d8
**Saving Throws:** Dexterity, Charisma
**Armor Proficiency:** Light armor only
**Weapon Proficiency:** Simple weapons only
**Spellcasting:** Full caster progression; prepared spells using Charisma

### Bardic Inspiration

The signature Bard feature. A number of uses per long rest equal to Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

- **Die Scale:** d6 (L1–4) → d8 (L5–9) → d10 (L10–14) → d12 (L15+)
- **Usage:** As a bonus action, grant an ally within 60 feet a die they can add to one d20 test (Attack, Ability Check, Saving Throw) within 1 hour
- **Font of Inspiration (L5):** Bardic Inspiration recharges on Short Rest. Additionally, the Bard can spend a spell slot to recover 1 use of Bardic Inspiration.

### Expertise

Bards gain Expertise in 2 skills at L1, allowing them to add doubled proficiency bonus to those checks. At L9, they gain Expertise in 2 additional skills. Expertise represents mastery far beyond typical training.

### Jack of All Trades

At L2, the Bard adds half their proficiency bonus (rounded down) to ability checks they make that don't already include their proficiency bonus. This is a constant passive boost to non-proficient checks.

## Spell Slots & Preparation

Full caster progression identical to Wizard. Prepared spells scale from 4 at L1 to 22 at L20, calculated as: Bard Level + Charisma Modifier (minimum 4 at L1).

From L10 onward (Magical Secrets), prepared spells can be drawn from Bard, Cleric, Druid, or Wizard spell lists instead of only the Bard list.

## Feature Progression

| Level | Feature |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bardic Inspiration, Spellcasting (Charisma) |
| 2 | Expertise (2 skills), Jack of All Trades |
| 3 | Subclass (College choice) |
| 5 | Font of Inspiration |
| 7 | Countercharm (reaction: reroll Charmed/Frightened save with Advantage) |
| 9 | Expertise (2 additional skills) |
| 10 | Magical Secrets (prepare from any of Bard/Cleric/Druid/Wizard spell lists) |
| 18 | Superior Inspiration (regain Bardic Inspiration to minimum 2 on Initiative roll) |
| 20 | Words of Creation (always prepare Power Word Heal and Power Word Kill; each spell targets up to 2 creatures) |

## Design Philosophy

The Bard is a skill-monkey support caster with reliable bonus action economy (Bardic Inspiration). Expertise represents unmatched mastery in chosen skills. The Charisma saves and performance-oriented presentation reinforce the "face" and diplomat archetype. ^[inferred]
