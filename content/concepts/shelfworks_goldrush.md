## Overview

Following [[catarina_davirelli]]'s successful reverse-engineering of [[antheri_engineering|Antheri components]] in 1488DR, a wave of prospectors, scholars, and criminal contractors descended on the [[the_antheri_ruins|Shelfworks]] above the Antheri Ruins. Named retroactively as the "Shelfworks Goldrush."

Catarina herself did not participate — she returned to [[calveno]] and put her workshop into production.

## Significance

The goldrush is the proximate cause of increased activity around the Maw's upper rim in the years before the campaign. The Shelfworks sit at 60–200 feet depth — accessible to surface divers and salvagers — making the goldrush physically feasible. ^[inferred] Whatever was recoverable from the accessible sections has likely been heavily picked over by 1495DR. ^[inferred]
