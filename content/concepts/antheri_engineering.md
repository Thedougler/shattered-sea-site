## Overview

[[the_antheri]]'s engineering tradition exceeds anything in current use in 1495DR, despite the civilization vanishing in -495DR — nearly 2,000 years prior. Their known capabilities:

- **Corrosion-proof composite stone** — structural material that resists seawater degradation
- **Living machinery** — mechanical or magical systems that remain functional millennia after construction
- **Underwater architecture** — building at depth, oriented around and into [[the_drowned_maw]] trench

Their most ambitious construction — [[the_antheri_ruins]] — descends directly down the Maw trench toward the planar fissure, to depths no living person has reached.

## Status in 1495DR

Components recovered from the Shelfworks section of the ruins can still function. [[catarina_davirelli]] reverse-engineered several in 1488DR, creating the [[clockwork_owl_familiar]] and accepting custom commissions like the [[salvaged_antheri_plate]]. Her work triggered the [[shelfworks_goldrush]] — evidence that functional Antheri tech is rare and extremely valuable. ^[inferred]
