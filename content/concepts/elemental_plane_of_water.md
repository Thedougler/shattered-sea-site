## Overview

A plane of existence connected to [[the_shattered_sea]] via the widening planar fissure at the floor of [[the_drowned_maw]]. [[leviathan]] has already crossed through this connection. Whether the Elemental Plane of Water is the origin of Leviathan or merely a transit layer between something deeper is unknown. ^[ambiguous]
